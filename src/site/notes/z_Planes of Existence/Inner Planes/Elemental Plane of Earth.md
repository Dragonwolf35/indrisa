The [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Elemental Plane of Earth\|Elemental Plane of Earth]] is a chain of mountains rising higher than any mountain range on the Material Plane. It has no sun of its own, and no air surrounds the peaks of its highest mountains. Most visitors to the plane arrive by the way of vast caverns that honeycomb the mountains.
