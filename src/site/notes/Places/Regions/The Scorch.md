# Description
The Scorch hits with a physical, suffocating wall of heat so absolute that it boils the moisture from the eyes and fractures the sanity of even fire-blooded tieflings. Rising from the shimmering, heat-warped horizon of this brutal wasteland is the continent's capital — a colossal, ancient bastion of the Material Plane that stubbornly survived the original planar shattering. Yet, despite housing the largest beacon of civilization in the realm, the surrounding expanse of cracked, sun-bleached hardpan remains a relentlessly hostile death trap, daring travellers to survive a desert that actively burns away both flesh and mind.
