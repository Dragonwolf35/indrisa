# Description
The Whispering Gardens plunge travellers into a perpetual, humid emerald twilight, where a suffocating dense canopy blocks the sky and the wind rustles the foliage into a constant, haunting murmur. Deep within this oppressive green labyrinth lies a sudden sanctuary of civilization clinging to the shores of [[The Jade Basin\|The Jade Basin]], a massive freshwater reservoir bursting with vibrant life. Yet, the true draw of the jungle lies far beyond the safety of the lake's settlement; whispers among the locals speak of the deep, untamed forest, where prospectors risk their lives hunting for Heatleaf — a rare, crimson-veined timber that radiates an eternal, smouldering warmth to the touch.

## Seasonal Effect
### Spring — Spore Bloom
A damp, magical spring causes giant blossoms to open throughout the labyrinthine gardens. Instead of heavy rain, precipitation comes as persistent, warm dew and drifting spore-mists that obscure vision beyond 30 feet.

### Summer — Stagnant Canopy
The air inside the Gardens becomes heavy, warm, and thick with sweet, nauseating floral scents. Rain falls as a soft, warm drizzle, filtered through massive leaves and dense ivy arbores.

### Autumn — Whispering Leaf
The leaves and vines dry out, creaking and rustling against one another in the light autumnal breezes. A thick layer of decaying, multicolored compost covers the ground, concealing hidden roots, pit traps, and ancient stone ruins.

### Winter — Frozen Glass
Frost coats the crystalline structures, petrified vines, and evergreen canopies, creating an environment that looks like delicate spun glass. The cold is dry and still, making every snapped twig echo like a gunshot through the silent, frozen hedges.
