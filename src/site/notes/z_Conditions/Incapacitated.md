While you have the [[z_Conditions/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] condition, you experience the following effects.

***Inactive.*** You can't take any Action, Bonus Action, or Reaction.

***No Concentration.*** Your [[z_Rules/Concentration\|Concentration]] is broken.

***Speechless.*** You can't speak.

***Surprised.*** If you're [[z_Conditions/Incapacitated\|incapacitated]] when you roll [[z_Rules/Initiative\|Initiative]], you have Disadvantage on the roll.
