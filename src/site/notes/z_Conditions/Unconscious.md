While you have the [[z_Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] condition, you experience the following effects.

***Inert.*** You have the [[z_Conditions/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] and [[z_Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] conditions, and you drop whatever you're holding. When this condition ends, you remain [[z_Conditions/Prone\|Prone]].

***Speed 0.*** Your speed is 0 and can't increase.

***Attacks Affected.*** Attack rolls against you have Advantage.

***Saving Throws Affected.*** You automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws.

***Automatic Critical Hits.*** Any attack roll that hits you is a [[z_Rules/Critical Hit\|Critical Hit]] if the attacker is within 5 feet of you.

***Unaware.*** You're unaware of your surroundings.
