During downtime, your characters will obviously have spent time coming up with new ideas and combat manoeuvres. But you as players, obviously don't. So, at the start of every round, players will get a 5 minute break to discuss tactics, stretch their legs, and be on their phone.

Thereafter, each player gets 1 minute to specify what they want to use their action and bonus action on.
