# Description
The Green Expanse is an endless, disorienting ocean of waist-high verdant grass that ripples and crashes under the wind like roaring ocean waves. With no natural landmarks to break the horizon, travellers are left entirely exposed beneath a massive, crushing expanse of sky, deafened by the constant, howling gale. The true danger lies hidden within the deep grass — not just the massive, unseen predators that stalk through the stalks, but the unpredictable Razor-Gales: sudden, localized windstorms moving so impossibly fast that they can strip a wooden wagon to splinters and flay an unprotected traveller in seconds.

## Seasonal Effect
### Spring — The Rolling Green
The plains are lush, vibrant, and bursting with life. Rain falls in steady, rolling bands that you can see coming from miles away. The winds are brisk but pleasant, keeping the sky moving with fast, patchy clouds.

### Summer — The Thunderheads
Days are hot and baked under a massive, unblinking sun. While rain is infrequent, when it happens, it is terrifying. Massive, anvil-shaped thunderheads form in the afternoons, unleashing brief but explosive downpours and lethal lightning strikes with zero cover available for the party.

### Autumn — The Howling Grass
The grasses dry out and turn golden-brown. The defining feature of autumn is the wind — relentless, howling gales that flatten the tall grass and make hearing anything over a shout nearly impossible. Precipitation is light, often just brief, dusty squalls.

### Winter — The White Expanse
Brutal and unforgiving. Without trees to act as windbreaks, the gales drive the snow horizontally, creating blinding whiteouts and massive snowdrifts. The temperature drops rapidly, and the wind chill makes it feel significantly colder than the thermometer reads.
