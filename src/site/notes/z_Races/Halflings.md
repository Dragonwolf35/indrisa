# Description
Halflings are perhaps the friendliest beings you will come across as they're known for their great spirits even in the most dire of circumstances. Although they're carefree, brave and curious, most of them are content with a life full of simple pleasures. As long as they have great company and the freedom to pursue their dreams, a halfling will be happy as can be even within the relative confinement of their own small community.

But their curiosity and bravery can make them great adventurers despite their tendency to avoid risky situations. Their small size often helps them avoid such situations, though they don't like being judged purely based on their height. As one may find out, halflings are capable of great feats regardless of their size.

# Cultural Quirks
Halflings are deeply tied to family and comfort, but because many of them are historically nomadic or tightly packed into small burrows, they don't build statues or paint large portraits. Instead, they weave their history into massive, communal "family quilts." Every major life event — a birth, a marriage, a great harvest, or surviving a harsh winter—is commemorated with a specific patch.

Older halfling families sleep under quilts that weigh fifty pounds and are fifty feet long. Stealing or damaging a family's fabric is considered a crime worse than murder, and offering to sew a patch for an outsider is the highest form of adoption.
