A volcanic mountain dominates each of the four layers of [[z_Planes of Existence/Outer Planes/Gehenna\|Gehenna]], and lesser volcanic earthbergs drift in the air and smash into the greater mountains. The rocky slopes of the plane make movement difficult and dangerous. The ground inclines at least 45 degrees almost everywhere. In places, steep cliffs and deep canyons present more challenging obstacles. Hazards include volcanic fissures that vent noxious fumes or searing flames.

[[z_Planes of Existence/Outer Planes/Gehenna\|Gehenna]] is the birthplace of yugoloths, greedy and selfish Fiends that dwell here in great numbers.
