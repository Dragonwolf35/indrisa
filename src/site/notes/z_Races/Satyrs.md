# Description
Satyrs are driven by their search for every possible pleasure life has to offer. They're virtually always in a great mood, which can be offputting to other races, but satyrs don't let this hinder them. To satyrs, life is a gift of the god meant to be enjoyed to its fullest, so those races and individuals who dedicate their lives to imposing laws, order and means to decipher the universe are often scoffed at and mocked. There's no time like the present after all, and every moment has something to offer. The song of a bird, a summer's breeze, the sweet scent of freshly baked goods, there's always something for a satyr to enjoy.

# Cultural Quirks
Satyrs are fey creatures of revelry, wine, and music. They prioritize the "vibe" above all else and lack the emotional bandwidth for prolonged grief.

They view negative emotions (sadness, mourning, stress) not just as unpleasant, but as a literal, highly contagious disease.

If a party member is genuinely sad, Satyrs will treat them like a plague victim. They will cover their mouths, put on plague-doctor masks, and try to aggressively "cure" the person with medical-grade partying, forcibly playing lutes at them and pouring wine down their throat until the "sickness" passes.
