###### Damage Types and Objects.

Objects have immunity to Poison and Psychic damage. The DM might decide that some damage types are more or less effective against an object.
###### Damage Threshold.

Big objects, such as castle walls, often have extra resilience represented by a damage threshold.
###### No Ability Scores.

An object lacks ability scores unless a rule assigns a score to the object. Without ability scores, an object can't make ability checks, and it fails all saving throws.½
