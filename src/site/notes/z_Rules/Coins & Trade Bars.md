# Coins
You will often find coins on your travels and you can spend those coins in shops, inns, and other businesses. I hear you already asking "why do all these civilizations spanning across centuries all use the same currency?" Don't worry about it!


| Coin                | Value in GP |
| ------------------- | ----------- |
| Copper Piece (CP)   | 1/100       |
| Silver Piece (SP)   | 1/10        |
| Electrum Piece (EP) | 1/2         |
| Gold Piece (GP)     | 1           |
| Platinum Piece (PP) | 10          |

<br>

Of course, with each coin weighing ⅓ of an ounce, so 50 coins weighing a pound, carrying around 50,000 copper pieces would be ***heavy*** —so it's wise to occasionally exchange your coins, perhaps into Trade Bars.

# Trade Bars
Because large numbers of coins can be difficult to transport and account for, many merchants prefer to use trade bars — ingots of precious metals and alloys (usually silver). These bars are valued by weight, as shown below.


| Bar                | Value  | Dimensions                              |
| ------------------ | ------ | --------------------------------------- |
| 2-pound silver bar | 10 GP  | 5 in. long x 2 in. wide x 1/2 in. thick |
| 5-pound silver bar | 25 GP  | 6 in. long x 2 in. wide x 1in. thick    |
| 5-pound gold bar   | 250 GP | 5 in. long x 2 in. wide x ¾ in. thick   |
