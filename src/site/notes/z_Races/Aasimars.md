# Description
Aasimar are humans with a celestial heritage, which shows through glowing eyes, and the ability to release energy in the form of two incorporeal wings.  
Their celestial heritage also gave them an almost destiny-like path in life, one of guardianship, protection against evil, and just generally being a force of good. Some see this as a burden however, and will deviate from this path.

While most Aasimar who deviate from their given path tend to do so by simply being more reclusive, some become forces of evil. Perhaps out of spite or perhaps because they are descendant of humans, possess free will, and are mortal. Either way they become a terrible force to be reckoned with.

Aasimar are usually born of human parents, and as such have human names. Some have more angelic names to reflect their origin though — the angelic names are mostly melodic, but strong sounding. There are subtle differences between male and female names, but some names could definitely be seen as unisex.

# Cultural Quirks
Because the gods are dead, temples are just empty stone. The only places where divine magic still dimly flickers are within the Aasimar themselves.

When mingling with other races, Aasimar act as, and are treated as, living mailboxes for the divine. It is a common cultural courtesy for an Aasimar to carry a small, ash-filled censor or an alms bowl. When commoners (humans, halflings, etc.) feel desperate or nostalgic, they will walk right up to an Aasimar in a tavern or on the street, press a coin or a written prayer into their hands, and ask them to "pass it on."

Aasimar accept this as a solemn duty, patiently listening to the prayers of strangers while they eat their lunch or shop for gear. They believe their magic remains active only because they carry the collective, lingering faith of the world's populace on their shoulders.
