# Description
The Genasi are the children of mortals and genies. They possess the powers of the Elemental Planes, have characteristics of both their mortal and genie heritage, but often have no place they can truly call home.

Most Genasi are ignored by their genie parents, who see them as a mistake. Their oddly coloured skins make them stand out among regular mortals despite taking some of the features of those parents, like pointy ears, or short statures.

Genasi usually take or receive a name belonging to the culture of their mortal parent, so a Genasi born of a genie and an elf would likely receive an elvish name. Some pick a name later in life to reflect their elemental heritage however, names like 'Cinder', 'Gust', 'Flow', and 'Pebble'.

# Cultural Quirks
Genasi are living conduits to the Elemental Planes. While they look mostly humanoid, the elemental magic inside them is incredibly volatile and tied directly to their emotions.

They cannot hide how they are feeling because their immediate environment physically reacts to their emotional state.

A depressed Water Genasi will cause the tavern room to grow incredibly damp, with mould sprouting in the corners. An angry Fire Genasi will accidentally scorch the upholstery of the chair they are sitting on. Tavern keepers charge Genasi a hefty "magical insurance" deposit before renting them a room.
