# Description
The Githyanki are born of conflict and seem to be unable to escape it. Though they have escaped their mind flayer oppressors, a civil war followed soon after and split the Gith up into Githyanki and Githzerai. The Githyanki remain in their original home while the Githzerai relocated to the Limbo plane. In their original home, the Githyanki use mind flayer and elder brains to fuel the Throne of Bones upon which their queen, Vlaakith, sits and reigns supreme.

All Githyanki are raised from birth to be loyal warriors to Vlaakith. Their training is brutal and a mixture of combat training and cult-like education, and raids are performed on a somewhat regular basis to both ensure world domination, Vlaakith's reign and keep the Githyanki occupied. Without raids, the Githyanki often seek pleasure in decadence, despite Vlaakith trying to promote more fulfilling purposes in life. While raids would offer an easy distraction, Vlaakith fears they would come at either the cost of her best warriors and leave her vulnerable or worse: make her best warriors powerful enough to challenge her reign.

# Cultural Quirks
Githyanki hail from the Astral Sea, a silver void where time simply does not pass. People don't age, food doesn't rot, and things don't decay.

When they travel to the Material Plane, they are deeply, existentially unnerved by the concept of aging, spoiling, and rust.

To cope, Githyanki raiding parties will magically preserve or completely silver-plate absolutely mundane items just to stop them from decaying. A Githyanki camp will feature perfectly preserved, silver-dipped apples that are completely inedible, but bring the Githyanki deep psychological comfort because they are "safe from time."
