[[z_Planes of Existence/Outer Planes/Ysgard\|Ysgard]] is a rugged realm of soaring mountains, deep fjords, and windswept battlefields, with summers that are long and hot and winters that are cold and unforgiving. Its continents float above oceans of volcanic rock, below which are enormous icy caverns that hold entire kingdoms of giants, humans, dwarves, gnomes, and other beings. Heroes come to [[z_Planes of Existence/Outer Planes/Ysgard\|Ysgard]] to test their mettle not only against the plane itself, but also against giants, dragons, and other mighty creatures across [[z_Planes of Existence/Outer Planes/Ysgard\|Ysgard]]'s vast terrain.
