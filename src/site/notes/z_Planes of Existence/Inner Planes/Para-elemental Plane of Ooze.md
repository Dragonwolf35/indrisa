The border region between the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Elemental Plane of Water\|Elemental Plane of Water]] and the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Elemental Plane of Earth\|Elemental Plane of Earth]] is a horrid swamp where gnarled trees and thick, stinging vines grow from the dense muck and slime. Here and there on the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Para-elemental Plane of Ooze\|Para-elemental Plane of Ooze]] (also called the Swamp of Oblivion), stagnant lakes and pools play host to thickets of weeds and monstrous swarms of mosquitoes. The few settlements here consist of wooden structures suspended above the muck on platforms between trees. Visitors to the plane have sometimes tried elevating houses on poles stuck in the mud, but since no solid earth underlies the muck, even such structures eventually sink.

It is said that any object cast into the Swamp of Oblivion can't be found again for at least a century. Now and then, a desperate soul casts an Artifact of power into this place, keeping it away from the rest of the multiverse for a time. The promise of powerful magic lures adventurers to brave the monstrous insects and hags of the swamp.
