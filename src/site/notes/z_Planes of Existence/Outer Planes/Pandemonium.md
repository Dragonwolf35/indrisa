[[z_Planes of Existence/Outer Planes/Pandemonium\|Pandemonium]] is a plane of overwhelming chaos, a great mass of rock riddled with tunnels carved by howling winds. It is cold, noisy, and dark, with no natural light. Wind quickly extinguishes nonmagical open flames such as torches or campfires. Only by yelling, and even then only to a maximum distance of 10 feet.

Most of the plane's inhabitants are creatures that were banished to the plane with no hope of escape. The incessant winds force them to take shelter in places where the howls of the wind sounds like distant cries of torment.
