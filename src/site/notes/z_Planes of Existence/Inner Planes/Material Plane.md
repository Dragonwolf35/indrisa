Worlds of the Material Plane were once infinitely diverse, but on a fatal day, they were all brought back to the legends of the primordial state, a single reality called the First World, but now dubbed the Last World.

Within the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Material Plane\|Material Plane]], a large explosion occurred, tearing a hole through the fabric of the planes, bringing all planes of existence, both inner and outer, into one. As a result, the Material Plane has transformed, absorbing and mutating aspects, locations, creatures, and items of great power into itself.

While many perished on the first day of the Last World, people have overcome difficulties and settled, rebuilding cities, building new ones, lived their lives. Now millennia later, this story begins.
