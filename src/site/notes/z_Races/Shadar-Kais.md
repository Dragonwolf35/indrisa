# Description
The Shadar-kai are elf-like fey beings who worship the Raven Queen within Shadowfell, though other origin stories float about as well. They usually have grey skin and dark hair, though there are variations on this as well. [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Shadowfell\|Shadowfell]] turned them into beings devoid of most emotions, though anger still coursed through their veins. They were also cursed by a "shadow curse", which would sap parts of their soul whenever they were incapacitated or close to death. While possible to resist this pull, once part of a soul has been pulled it is forever lost to the Plane of Shadow and future pulls are all the harder to resist. The curse drained their will to live as well, making most Shadar-kai live with an often dangerous disregard for their own safety, though many do strife for glory in order to reach some form of immortality after their death through the stories told of their deeds.

# Cultural Quirks
Because they serve a realm that hoards memories and emotional resonance, Shadar-Kai have completely abandoned the concept of inherent monetary value. To them, gold and gems are just boring, shiny rocks.

They value objects strictly based on the emotional "weight" or history attached to them. A flawless, newly minted platinum coin is considered worthless garbage because it has no story. However, a cracked wooden mug that a dwarven king once threw at his treacherous brother is viewed as a priceless, breath-taking artefact.

Shadar-Kai are beautifully frustrating to do business with. They will carelessly leave their actual gold coins unattended on a tavern table, but will aggressively try to haggle a party member for their worn-out, muddy boots because "they are saturated with the exquisite misery of a thousand miles." A Shadar-Kai character might try to pay a bewildered tavern keeper by solemnly handing them a rusty, bent spoon and saying, "A weeping widow dropped this in a gutter thirty years ago. Keep the change."
