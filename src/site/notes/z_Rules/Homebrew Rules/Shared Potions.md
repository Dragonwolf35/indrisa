Don't you just hate it when your party member has all the potions, and yet here you are, close to death, and there they are, across the room, surrounded by enemies? Fret not young (or old) one. With this nifty potion-only dimensional storage, as long as one person has a potion on them, everyone else can use it as well!

I.e., if person A has a potion, Person B, C, & D also have it in their inventory.
