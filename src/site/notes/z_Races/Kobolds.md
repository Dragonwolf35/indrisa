# Description
Kobolds are small, reptilian creatures who live in the underground tunnels they skillfully create. These underground homes offer much needed protection from other creatures, as many of them would enslave, eradicate, or otherwise harm kobolds if given the chance.

Despite their small stature kobolds are dedicated workers and fierce opponents, at least when they're able to work together. They wish nothing more than to live in peace, dig tunnels, and raise their young within their underground homes.

# Cultural Quirks
Kobolds are fiercely communal and possess a pathological, biological need to serve a dragon. Without a dragon to worship, a Kobold tribe undergoes a collective existential crisis.

If a Kobold (or a group of them) is separated from a dragon, they will panic-adopt an exceptionally tall, strong, or impressive party member as their "Surrogate Dragon" without asking permission.

The chosen party member will wake up to find shiny garbage, stolen silverware, and polished rocks left at the foot of their bedroll. Eventually, the Kobolds will start building crude, heavily trapped shrines to the party member in the basement of whatever inn they are staying at.
