# Description
The Iron Spines pierce the sky like jagged, soot-stained teeth, their peaks constantly veiled in a choking haze of ash and sulphur. Deep within this treacherous, rumbling range lies the continent's largest dwarven settlement, a hidden bastion carved directly into the stone beside a bizarre, unyielding forest of Iron Wood. Navigating these crags is a terrifying gamble against the earth itself; the ground frequently trembles and splits as sudden volcanic eruptions paint the slopes in liquid fire, leaving travellers to wonder if the cataclysms are mere geological disasters, or the restless stirring of colossal, ancient beasts waking in the deep.
