# Description
Boundary-End is a localized apocalypse — a jagged, impossible ring surrounding the continent's central scar where the primal forces of creation violently bleed into reality. To stand upon the crater's brim is to be deafened by the eternal, grinding roar of the elemental and para-elemental planes colliding in a permanent state of catastrophic war. Here, the laws of physics are entirely shattered; travellers must navigate a nightmare of erupting magma-glass, flash-freezing blizzards, suffocating primordial ooze, and crushing cyclonic winds that can shift in a single heartbeat. It is a hostile, terrifying borderland, serving as a brutal reminder that the gods are dead and the fabric of the world is violently unravelling.
