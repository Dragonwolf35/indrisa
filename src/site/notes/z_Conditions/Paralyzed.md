While you have the [[z_Conditions/Paralyzed\|Paralyzed]] condition, you experience the following effects.

***Incapacitated.*** You have the [[z_Conditions/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] condition.

***Speed 0.*** Your Speed is 0 and can't increase.

***Saving Throws Affected.*** You automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws.

***Attacks Affected.*** Attack rolls against you have Advantage.

***Automatic Critical Hits.*** Any attack roll that hits you is a [[z_Rules/Critical Hit\|Critical Hit]] if the attacker is within 5 feet of you.
