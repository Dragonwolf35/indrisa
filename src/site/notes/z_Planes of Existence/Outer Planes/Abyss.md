The Abyss embodies all that is perverse, gruesome, and chaotic. Its virtually endless layers spiral downward into ever more appalling forms.

Each layer of the [[z_Planes of Existence/Outer Planes/Abyss\|Abyss]] boasts a horrific environment that is harsh and inhospitable to mortals. Each layer also reflect the entropic nature of the [[z_Planes of Existence/Outer Planes/Abyss\|Abyss]]. Much of the plane seems to be in a decaying, crumbling, or corroded state, and its corrupting affects visitors.
