The boundary between the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Elemental Plane of Earth\|Elemental Plane of Earth]] and the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Elemental Plane of Fire\|Elemental Plane of Fire]] is a great range of volcanic mountains. The [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Para-elemental Plane of Magma\|Para-elemental Plane of Magma]], also called the fountains of Creation, is home to azers, fire giants, and red dragons, as well as creatures from the neighbouring planes. Lava flows down the slopes of these mountains toward the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Elemental Plane of Fire\|Elemental Plane of Fire]].
