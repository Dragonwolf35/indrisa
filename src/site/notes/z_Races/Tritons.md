# Description
Tritons are aquatic humanoids with webbed feet, webbed hands, blue skins, and colourful hair. They're the protectors of not just the ocean depths, but, if you asked them, of the entire world.

While there is some arrogance at play, much of which comes from living such isolated lives, they are genuinely protective of those in need, and of the entire world. Their arrogant and sometimes self-righteous behaviour can be off putting to some, but the tritons mean well. They're simply not used to the way life works outside of their deep sea homes.

Triton names are melodic, but do have some harsher tones as well. Female names always end in -yn, and male names always end in -s. Their surnames always end in -ath, and tend to be much longer and complex than their personal names. Male names also seem to always be 2 syllables long, while female names can be 2 to 3 syllables long.

# Cultural Quirks
Hailing from the deep ocean where water is an omnipresent, shared environment, Tritons have absolutely no concept of "contained" or "owned" liquids.

A Triton views all liquids as an open-source, communal resource. They will casually drink from a stranger's goblet at a tavern, wash their hands in a town's decorative fountain, or try to swim in a noble's private bath. When surface dwellers get angry at them for "stealing" a drink, Tritons view the surface dwellers as insanely greedy water-hoarders.

Adventuring with a Triton requires extreme vigilance over one's inventory, especially glass vials. Party members quickly learn to hide their magical potions and dangerous poisons, as a Triton will happily chug a wizard's expensive _Potion of Invisibility_ just because they were feeling a bit parched. In public, the party usually has to assign a designated "handler" to follow the Triton around taverns, hurriedly tossing gold coins onto the tables of angry, bewildered patrons whose ale the Triton casually downed while walking past.
