> [!Narration] The Lunar Bodies
> Look to the night sky and you will see the unbroken gaze of the old world. Two celestial bodies, forever locked in a blinking cycle — as one opens in waxing light, the other closes in waning shadow. The scholars of this age believe them to be mere rocks caught in the world's gravity. They are not. They are a constant, unblinking surveillance, watching the fracture of the planes.
