The [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Feywild\|Feywild]], also called the Plane of Faerie, is a land of soft lights and wonder, a place of music and magic. The plane responds to unfettered emotion: flowers turn and tremble in the presence of a heated argument, grass withers under the feet of one who seethes with malice, and birds chirp merrily in the presence of those who are joyous and squawk angrily at those who are dour.

Time and distance in the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Feywild\|Feywild]] are mutable, as the plane's geography. Roads are uncommon, and the ones that exist changes as frequently as the land around themselves. [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Feywild\|Feywild]] natives are accustomed to the plane's mutability, but it can be terribly disorienting to visitors.

The [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Feywild\|Feywild]] exists in parallel to the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Material Plane\|Material Plane]] as an alternate dimension that occupies the same cosmological space. When moving from the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Material Plane\|Material Plane]] to the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Feywild\|Feywild]], travellers usually find themselves in a location similar to the one they left, but more marvellous and magical — and often more vibrant and colourful, too. Adventurers climbing a volcano on the Material Plane might suddenly find themselves scaling a Feywild Mountain topped with skyscraper-sized crystals that glow with internal fire. Leaving behind a wide and muddy river on the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Material Plane\|Material Plane]], characters might appear beside a clear and winding brook whose waters glitter like diamonds in the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Feywild\|Feywild]]. In the heart of a dismal marsh might lie a portal leading to a vast bog filled with eerie lights and sinister shapes twisting in the water. And moving to the Feywild from old ruins on the Material Plane might put a traveller at the door of an archfey's castle.
