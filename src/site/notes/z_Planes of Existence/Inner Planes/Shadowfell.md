The [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Shadowfell\|Shadowfell]] also called the Plane of Shadow, is a gloomy dimension whose sky is a black vault with neither sun nor stars.

The [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Shadowfell\|Shadowfell]] overlaps the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Material Plane\|Material Plane]] in much the same way as the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Feywild\|Feywild]]. Aside from the bleak landscape, it appears similar to the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Material Plane\|Material Plane]]. Travellers from the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Material Plane\|Material Plane]] who enter the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Shadowfell\|Shadowfell]] often observe landmarks similar to the world they left, but distorted and often sinister. A mountain on the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Material Plane\|Material Plane]] might be replaced in the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Shadowfell\|Shadowfell]] by a skull-shaped rock outcropping, a heap of rubble, or the crumbling ruin of a once-great castle. The forests of [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Shadowfell\|Shadowfell]] hold sinister-looking trees, their branches reaching out to snare travellers' cloaks, and their roots coiling to trip those who pass by.

Shadow dragons and Undead haunt this bleak plane, as do other creatures that thrive in the gloom, including cloakers and darkmantles.
