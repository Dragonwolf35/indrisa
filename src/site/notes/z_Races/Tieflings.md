# Description
Tieflings are the result of humans who made a deal with a demon or fiend. When those humans sought more power, demons and fiends answered.

Although the humans got their power, their appearance changed drastically. Horns, tails, sharp teeth, and red skin are some of the most prominent changes. What's perhaps worse is that their off-spring retained these features.

While the original tieflings were most definitely evil or at the very least had bad intentions, tieflings today don't necessarily have to be. While it's true there are plenty of tieflings who savour their dark heritage, others just wish to live a good, normal life. However, due to their appearance and heritage, many other races still look at tieflings with a lot of prejudice, which, although gradually fading, is strengthened by those who chose to relish in evil.

Life can be hard and cruel for a tieflings who wishes to help and do good, especially for those wishing to escape their heritage, but their pride, natural mystique, cunning, and self-reliance can help them quickly prove their worth.

# Cultural Quirks
Because Tieflings often face mistrust due to their infernal heritage, their given names or family names are sometimes sources of trauma or danger. To counter this, they reinvent themselves constantly.

Tieflings adopt a new "Virtue Name" (like _Hope, Carrion, Poetry_, or _Vengeance_) every time they survive a major life event, tragedy, or triumph. They don't replace the old names; they just add to them.

An elderly or incredibly successful Tiefling might have a name that takes a full minute to recite (e.g., _Sorrow-Turned-Ash-In-The-Winter-Storm-Bringer-Of-Light_). Introducing themselves to strangers with their full title is a test of patience to see who is actually worth talking to.
