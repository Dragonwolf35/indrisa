# Description
The Sun-Kissed Plateaus are a dizzying, sun-baked labyrinth of towering red-rock mesas and plunging, shadow-draped canyons. The heat here doesn't just beat down from the sky; it radiates brutally from the scorched stone beneath your boots, creating shimmering mirages that distort distances and ruin any sense of direction. Navigating this fractured landscape is a constant, terrifying balancing act, where a single misstep on the crumbling, wind-carved ledges promises a lethal drop, and taking a wrong turn into the sprawling network of identical, echoing ravines is an easy way to vanish forever.

## Seasonal Effect
### Spring — The Updraft
The most forgiving time on the plateaus. Warm thermals rise from the deep canyons, bringing a constant, refreshing breeze. The normally stark stone blooms rapidly with hardy, vibrant wildflowers. Rain is light but steady, often blowing horizontally due to the wind.

### Summer — The Sun-Hammer
The unobstructed sun bakes the stone expanses, turning the ground into a radiator. There is almost zero shade, making heat exhaustion a constant threat. While precipitation is rare, it arrives as sudden, violent monsoons that dump inches of water in minutes, creating lethal flash floods that cascade over the plateau edges like temporary waterfalls.

### Autumn — Amber Dust
The scrub vegetation dries to a crisp gold, and the air cools rapidly. The defining feature is the high, whistling wind that kicks up blinding dust devils across the flat expanses. With the humidity entirely gone, the night skies are breathtakingly clear and piercingly cold.

### Winter — Frost-Glaze
The plateaus see extreme temperature swings. At night, the temperature drops below freezing, coating the rocky edges in slick, treacherous black ice. However, the daytime sun is still intense enough to melt the frost by noon. Snow is rare, but when it falls, it is instantly blown into massive drifts against any ruins or rock formations.
