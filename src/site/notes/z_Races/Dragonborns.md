# Description
The dragonborn are proud, strong, and honorable. Honor is everything to the dragonborn, both in and out of battle. They will be respectful even of their enemies in battle, no matter how foul they may be. Although honesty is important to dragonborn as well, they don't care much for evil or good. They can be either, and it doesn't matter too much, as long as they don't stoop to dishonourable acts. Dragonborn hold no grudges towards an entire race, even if that race has committed atrocious acts. Instead, they judge on a per person basis. Every race has good and evil individuals, so a dragonborn will judge you based on your actions, rather than your race's history.

Like dragons, dragonborn were often mistaken for reptiles, but were in fact warm-blooded draconic creatures. In fact, the internal body temperature of the dragonborn was warmer than that of most similar races, being so hot as to seem feverish to the human touch. While this might seem disadvantageous, the lack of hair and large mouth allowed the dragonborn to displace body heat at an effective rate, meaning dragonborn were comfortable in cold climates while remaining no more vulnerable to heat than humans.

# Cultural Quirks
With their innate draconic pride and intense clan loyalty, Dragonborn place a heavy emphasis on their physical connection to their draconic ancestors.

As Dragonborn age and naturally shed their larger, hardened scales, they don't throw them away. They carve them with runes and incorporate them into their family's armour, jewellery, or weaponry.

A Dragonborn warrior is essentially wearing the history of their parents and grandparents into battle. To willingly give a shed scale to a non-Dragonborn is an intensely intimate gesture of brotherhood; to have one forcibly taken is an insult that demands a blood feud.
