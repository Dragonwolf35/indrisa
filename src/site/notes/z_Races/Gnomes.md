# Description
Gnomes are true tricksters. Their small size and sense of humour are often used to deceive both friend and enemy, as well as to hide their true intentions. However, although they do have their suspicions of taller races, most Gnomes mean no harm and only wish to joke around.

Gnomes are also very curious, which makes them great engineers as they're always trying out something new, even if it's just to see how other Gnomes or other races would react to their creations. This love of engineering and their love of gems has strengthened their relationship with dwarves, who also share a hatred for goblins, orcs, and other foul creatures.

Gnomes can be the perfect party companion for those with a sense of humor and for those in need of a master of stealth. Gnomes grow up with games of stealth, tricks, and mind games, all of which are valued highly among gnomes. So it's no surprise Gnome heroes usually aren't great warriors who vanquished a greater foe, but instead are those who escaped against near impossible odds, those who played the best and most intricate pranks, or those who could stealthily slip by an enemy's defences.

# Cultural Quirks
Gnomes are intensely curious tinkerers who hate being bored, but they also form deep, fast attachments to people and absolutely despise the concept of saying goodbye.

When a Gnome leaves home for a long journey, they refuse to leave their house empty. They build tiny, intricate clockwork "Echo-Boxes" and hide them in their friends' and families' homes.

These boxes randomly whir to life to play a recorded snippet of the Gnome's humming, a joke they told, or the sound of their footsteps. While gnomes find this deeply comforting, outsiders often find Gnomish villages incredibly haunted, as empty rooms constantly echo with the phantom noises of people who aren't there.
