# Description
The eladrin are a race of elves with a born interest in the arcane. However, they're not only masters of magic, but also of the sword. But despite their mastery of sword and magic, they tend to live secluded lives in their well protected cities. But there are still plenty of champions who choose to fight the evils that threaten the lands.

The eladrin are graceful, patient, and perceptive. Most of them love to explore, although the evil lurking in the lands puts some off of the idea to follow this passion. The Eldrin make great party companions. Their intellect is among the best of the humanoids, and their agility allows them to manoeuvre easily in rough terrain.

The Eladrin also require no sleep, and they rest differently from most other races. Instead of entering a state of sleep, the Eladrin enter a state of trance during which they're still aware of their surroundings, perfect for groups expecting an ambush at night.

# Cultural Quirks
Because their personalities and physical forms shift dramatically with the seasons or their emotional states, Eladrin have adapted to view their four seasonal aspects (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter) as socially and legally distinct individuals.

An Eladrin strictly compartmentalizes their promises, debts, and grudges based on which "season" they were when they made them. They consider it a severe breach of etiquette for outsiders to hold them accountable for the actions of their other seasons.

If an exuberant Spring Eladrin promises a dwarven merchant they will pay for a sword tomorrow, but wakes up the next day having shifted to a sullen Winter Eladrin, they will flatly refuse to pay the debt, viewing it as someone else's financial burden. To survive in mixed company without constantly being arrested for fraud, an Eladrin usually carries four color-coded journals, constantly checking to see what their "other selves" have agreed to and openly apologizing for the reckless, impulsive decisions of their Summer state.
