Initiative determines the order of turns during combat. When combat starts, every participant rolls Initiative; they roll a d4 and add/subtract their dexterity modifier (homebrew rule; see [[z_Rules/Homebrew Rules/Initiative Rolls\|Initiative Rolls]]) to determine their place.
