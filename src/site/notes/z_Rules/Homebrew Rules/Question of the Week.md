Between sessions, the DM will randomly enter a question into the group chat. These can be miniscule, like "what's your character's favourite food", or "what's something they enjoy doing in their downtime", but it could also be bigger, "who's someone that has wronged you in the past, and how?" or "how did your character feel the first time they killed someone?", or maybe something lore/game mechanic related, "where did you find this item" or "how do you calculate your spell save DC."

Your answer can vary, it can be text, voice note, a gif, just anything that gives the general idea.

Now here's the interesting bit. The first person to give a (satisfactory) answer, can, at any point during the next session, add +5 to any ability check, saving throw, or attack roll. Second person gets +4, third +3, and so on. Of course, if you don't answer, nada.
