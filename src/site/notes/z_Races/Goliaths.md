# Description
Goliaths are huge, humanoid creatures with an intense love for competition. Anything that can be seen as a challenge, will be seen as a challenge, and a goliath will gladly track the progress of the whole party, especially when that goliath is better than the others.

Their competitive and curious nature has been both a gift and a curse in their nomadic lifestyle. While on one hand it often leads to finding better lands and new food sources, it also makes them a bit reckless.

But goliaths have a great respect for the natural world, and both cherish and admire it. They even call upon the spirits of nature and their ancestors to aid them when in need.

Goliaths have 3 names. They start their lives with a first name and a clan name, but once they've come of age, a third name is given related to his or her most impressive deed thus far. This name can change throughout life, both for better and for worse. A better name could be given when a new deed is more impressive than the previous, or a worse name can be given as a type of punishment.

# Cultural Quirks
Goliaths live in harsh, unforgiving environments where a single lazy person can doom an entire tribe. Because of this, their culture is an extreme meritocracy.

They constantly measure their daily usefulness and keep a literal, physical tally of it on their belts using complex knots or carved beads.

A Goliath physically cannot relax. If they are sitting still at a tavern, they are doing frantic mental math to ensure they haven't "fallen behind" the rest of the party's contributions for the day. If they feel they are in a deficit, they will start aggressively chopping wood for the innkeeper or forcibly carrying the party's luggage just to balance their emotional ledger.
