On the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Para-elemental Plane of Ash\|Para-elemental Plane of Ash]], also called the Great Conflagration, howling winds from the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Elemental Plane of Air\|Elemental Plane of Air]] mix with the cinder storms and lava of the [[z_Planes of Existence/Inner Planes/Elemental Plane of Fire\|Elemental Plane of Fire]]. This plane is an endless storm of flame, smoke, and ash. The thick ash obscures sight beyond a few dozen feet, and the battering winds make travel difficult. Here and there, ash clusters into floating realms where outlaws and fugitives take shelter.
