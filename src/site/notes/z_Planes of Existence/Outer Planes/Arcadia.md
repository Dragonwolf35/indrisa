[[z_Planes of Existence/Outer Planes/Arcadia\|Arcadia]] thrives with orchards of perfectly lined trees, ruler-straight streams, orderly fields, immaculate roads, and cities laid out in geometrically pleasing shapes. The mountains bear no trace of erosion.

Night and day are determined by an orb that floats in the sky above both of Arcadia's Layers. Half of the orb radiates sunlight and brings about the day; the other half sheds moonlight and brings on the starry night. The orb rotates evenly without fail, spreading day and night across the entire plane.

The weather in Arcadia is governed by our allied demigods called the Storm Kings: the Could King, the Wind Queen, the Lightning King, and the Rain Queen. Each one lives in a castle surrounded by the type of weather that ruler controls.

[[z_Planes of Existence/Outer Planes/Arcadia\|Arcadia]] is suffused with a vigorous life energy that bestows great vitality on visitors.
