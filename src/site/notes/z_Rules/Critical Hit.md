 If you roll a 20 on the d20 for an attack roll, you score a [[z_Rules/Critical Hit\|Critical Hit]], and the attack hits regardless of any modifiers or the target's AC. A [[z_Rules/Critical Hit\|Critical Hit]] lets you roll extra dice for the attack's damage against the target. Roll all of the attack's damage dice twice and add them together. Then add any relevant modifiers.
