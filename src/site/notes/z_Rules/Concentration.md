Some spells and other effects require [[z_Rules/Concentration\|Concentration]] to remain active, as specified in their descriptions. If the effect has a maximum duration, the effect's description specifies how long the creator can concentrate on it: up to 1 minute, 1 hour, or some other duration. The creator can end [[z_Rules/Concentration\|Concentration]] at any time (no action required). The following factors break [[z_Rules/Concentration\|Concentration]].

###### Another Concentration Effect.
You lose [[z_Rules/Concentration\|Concentration]] on an effect the moment you start casting a spell that requires [[z_Rules/Concentration\|Concentration]] or activate another effect that requires [[z_Rules/Concentration\|Concentration]].

###### Damage
If you take damage, you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw to maintain [[z_Rules/Concentration\|Concentration]]. The DC equals 10 or half the damage taken (rounded down), whichever number is higher, up to a maximum DC of 30.

######  Incapacitated or Dead.
Your [[z_Rules/Concentration\|Concentration]] ends if you have the [[z_Conditions/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] condition or you die.
