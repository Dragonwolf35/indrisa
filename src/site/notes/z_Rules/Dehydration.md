A creature requires an amount of water per day based on its size, as shown in the Water Needs per Day table below. A creature that drinks less than half their required water for a day gains 1 exhaustion level at the day's end. Exhaustion caused by dehydration can't be removed until the creature drinks the full amount of water required for a day.

For reference, a waterskin holds up to half a gallon.

| Size   | Water      | Size       | Water      |
| ------ | ---------- | ---------- | ---------- |
| Tiny   | 1/4 gallon | Large      | 4 gallons  |
| Small  | 1 gallon   | Huge       | 16 gallons |
| Medium | 1 gallon   | Gargantuan | 64 gallons |
