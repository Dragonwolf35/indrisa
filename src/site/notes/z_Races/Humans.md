# Description
Humans come in many different shapes, types and sizes. They have countless different ambitions, dreams, and goals. What they lack in strength they make up for in determination, and while their hearts may not always be pure, many will do anything to get what their heart desires. As such, humans could make the perfect or worst party member. They could be a glorious leader or filthy backstabber. They could do what's best for them or what's best for the group.

# Cultural Quirks
Humans are notoriously short-lived compared to elves or dwarves, which gives them a frantic, desperate ambition to leave a legacy behind before they die.

Humans refuse to tear down the monuments, foundations, or achievements of their ancestors. Instead of clearing land to build something new, they just build _on top_ of it.

Human cities are literal, precarious layer cakes of architecture. You might find a modern bakery built into the mouth of a 300-year-old statue of a forgotten king. Their cities are labyrinthine, chaotic, and structurally terrifying to other races, but humans view it as "respecting their elders."
