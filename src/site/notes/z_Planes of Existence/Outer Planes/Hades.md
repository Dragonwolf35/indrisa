The layers of [[z_Planes of Existence/Outer Planes/Hades\|Hades]] are called the Three Glooms — places without joy, hope, or passion. A grey land with an ashen sky, [[z_Planes of Existence/Outer Planes/Hades\|Hades]] is the destination of many souls that are unclaimed by gods or Fiends. These souls become larvae and spend eternity in this place, which lacks a sun, a moon, stars, or seasons. Leaching away colour and emotion, the gloom on this plane is more than most visitors can stand.
