To make social encounters feel like actual conversations rather than a single pass/fail Persuasion roll, we are using a new system built around uncovering what an NPC actually cares about. Here is how it works:

- **The Clock (Patience):** Every NPC has a "Patience" level. Every argument or pitch you make ticks this clock down. If their Patience hits zero before you convince them, the conversation is over, and they walk away or shut you down.
- **The Goal (Interest):** To win the negotiation, you need to raise the NPC's "Interest" level. You do this by making compelling points and succeeding on your skill checks (like Persuasion, Deception, or Intimidation).
- **The Hidden Traits (Motivations & Pitfalls):** Every major NPC has two hidden **Motivations** (things they desire, value, or fear) and one **Pitfall** (a hard boundary, prejudice, or taboo topic).
    - _Aligning_ your argument with a Motivation grants a major bonus to your roll or raises Interest faster.
    - _Hitting_ their Pitfall instantly tanks the negotiation, resulting in immediate failure or a massive loss of Patience.
- **Discovery:** You don't have to guess blindly. Before making your main pitch, you can spend your time asking probing questions or using skills like Insight to figure out their Motivations and Pitfall.
- **The Roleplay Reward:** If you make an airtight argument that perfectly aligns with the NPC's worldview or the current situation, you do not need to roll the dice. Excellent roleplay and tactical points grant an automatic success for that argument.
