# Description
The Scorch hits with a physical, suffocating wall of heat so absolute that it boils the moisture from the eyes and fractures the sanity of even fire-blooded tieflings. Rising from the shimmering, heat-warped horizon of this brutal wasteland is the continent's capital — a colossal, ancient bastion of the Material Plane that stubbornly survived the original planar shattering. Yet, despite housing the largest beacon of civilization in the realm, the surrounding expanse of cracked, sun-bleached hardpan remains a relentlessly hostile death trap, daring travellers to survive a desert that actively burns away both flesh and mind.

## Seasonal Effect
### Spring — Howling Sands
The seasonal shift brings high pressure and violent, roaring winds. This is the season of massive sandstorms (haboobs) that can black out the sun, strip paint from wagons, and scour exposed skin. Travel is frequently delayed as dunes actively migrate across established trade routes.

### Summer — The Crucible
Unrelenting, lethal heat. The horizon constantly ripples with hallucinatory mirages. Traveling during midday without magical protection or extreme water rationing is a death sentence. Rain is practically non-existent, but on the rare chance it hits, it is a catastrophic, ground-shattering deluge that flash-floods the baked canyons within minutes.

### Autumn — Cooling Ash
The brutal daytime heat begins to break, making overland travel manageable again. The air is bone-dry, and the scrub brush becomes highly flammable. As the season progresses, the temperature swing between midday and midnight grows increasingly extreme.

### Winter — Freezing Dark
The desert is dangerously deceptive. While the days are mild, bright, and perfectly comfortable, the sand holds absolutely no heat. The moment the sun sets, temperatures plummet well below freezing. Unprepared travelers who expect a warm desert night frequently succumb to hypothermia before dawn.
