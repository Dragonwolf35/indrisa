# Description
Dwarves were once a proud united race, but years of wars with orcs and goblins, internal conflicts, and other struggles have dispersed them across the lands. These new lands have given them new appearances, though many still share the typical dwarven look, no matter their size, hair or skin color.  

Alongside their pride, dwarfs are known to be brave, stubborn, and tenacious. But, perhaps above all, they were known to be suspicious of others. A dwarf would not trust you unless you had proven beyond a shadow of a doubt you were worthy of their trust. Until then a dwarf will keep you at length, always prepared for the worst.  

Dwarves also keep a grudge. If you betray their trust, they will get even one way or another regardless of how big or small the betrayal of their trust truly was. This has also fueled their hatred for orcs and goblins, whom they will slay with pleasure. Orcs, goblins and various other races are considered to be a plague which must be purged, before they can grow in numbers and become overwhelming.



# Cultural Quirks
Dwarves have such a profound, spiritual connection to the earth and the forge that they don't view crafting as manufacturing; they view it as giving birth.

They believe that raw ore has a sleeping soul, and the heat of the forge wakes it up. Therefore, a forged weapon or piece of armour is essentially an adopted child.

Gruff, battle-hardened Dwarven warriors will unironically baby-talk their battle-axes while polishing them ("Who's a bloodthirsty little cleaver? Yes, you are!"). Selling a dwarven-made item to an outsider is treated like an adoption agency vetting process, and neglecting to oil your mail is viewed as child abuse.

Dwarves also believe that if they were to die not holding a stone, they shall be denied entry into their afterlife.
