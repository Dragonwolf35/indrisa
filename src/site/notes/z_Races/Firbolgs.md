# Description
Firbolgs are creatures in tune with nature. They consider themselves guardians and caretakers of forests, and will protect them with incredible ferocity, especially when provoked. They live off of the land, but only to the extent of what they need. Anything more is given back to the lands and animals around them.

As beings in tune with nature, they are natural druids. While not every Firbolg will become a druid, pretty much every Firbolg knows a few spells. It's simply part of their upbringing and culture as a whole.

# Cultural Quirks
Firbolgs are gentle giant custodians of the forest who believe their highest calling is to leave absolutely no trace of their existence behind.

They have a cultural aversion to standing out, taking credit, or being the center of attention. They view humility not as a virtue, but as a survival mechanism.

If you directly compliment a Firbolg or try to throw a celebration in their honor, they will suffer a complete social panic attack. They might try to cast _Invisibility_, physically hide behind a small tree, or aggressively try to redirect the credit to a nearby rock, the weather, or the party's pack mule.
