# Description
Salt spray and the rhythmic thunder of crashing surf define the Tide-Wash Coast, a jagged archipelago standing alone against the surrounding landlocked landscape. At its centre lies a single, sprawling island dominated by a stubborn agricultural village, where salt-rimed terraces of hardy crops cling to the sea-cliffs. Surrounding this lone pocket of civilization is a scattered halo of sporadic, craggy islets that shatter the incoming tides into a constant, blinding mist. The relentless, churning waters between these landmasses conceal shifting sandbars and razor-sharp reefs, making any voyage beyond the village's shallow docks a treacherous gamble against the sea.
