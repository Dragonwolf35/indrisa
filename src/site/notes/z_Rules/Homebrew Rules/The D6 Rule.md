Who hasn't experienced missing an enemy's AC after delivering a heart-wrenching monologue, only for your character's personal villain be slain by someone else? Fear not, the D6 Rule is here to help.

If, when playing, you do anything that fits narratively with your character, do a recap of last session (especially in-character), or anything that makes the session enjoyable for everyone, you get a d6.

"But I already have a d6, why do I need another one, I'm not casting fireball-" We're getting to that. At any point, you can use this d6 (or as many as you want, they stack in fact) on a d20 roll. Attack, save, skill check, doesn't matter. You add it to the roll. Only caveat is that you need to announce it BEFORE you roll.

And you just keep being lucky, because you even get to share them. Hell, give one player an 8d6 bonus to an attack roll just to make ***sure*** they finally roll above a 5!
