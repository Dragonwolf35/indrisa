# Description
Salt spray and the rhythmic thunder of crashing surf define the Tide-Wash Coast, a jagged archipelago standing alone against the surrounding landlocked landscape. At its centre lies a single, sprawling island dominated by a stubborn agricultural village, where salt-rimed terraces of hardy crops cling to the sea-cliffs. Surrounding this lone pocket of civilization is a scattered halo of sporadic, craggy islets that shatter the incoming tides into a constant, blinding mist. The relentless, churning waters between these landmasses conceal shifting sandbars and razor-sharp reefs, making any voyage beyond the village's shallow docks a treacherous gamble against the sea.

## Seasonal Effect
### Spring — Salt Mist
The warming sea air colliding with cold winter tides creates massive banks of dense salt fog that swallow cliffs and harbors for days at a time. Ships are grounded, and light drizzles keep armor constantly slick with rust-inducing sea air.

### Summer — Coastal Squall
Balmy and pleasant for the most part, though afternoon heat triggers sudden, localized coastal gales. These squalls dump heavy rain for an hour before blowing out to sea, leaving behind hot, humid sea air.

### Autumn — Gale Season
High winds sweep off the water, kicking up massive breakers that batter coastal roads and cliffs. Rain falls in long, grey curtains, and nightfall brings damp cold that makes fires difficult to keep lit near the shoreline.

### Winter — Frosted Foam
Temperatures rarely drop far below freezing thanks to the ocean's warmth, but hurricane-force sea winds drive freezing spray inland, coating cliffs, sea towers, and rigging in thick layers of salt-ice.
