# Description
Elves are one with nature, and will do anything they can to protect it. Their archery and tactical skills are among the best, which they gladly use in combination with their often superior agility and cunning to protect what they love.  
Elves are uncomfortable in (civilized) cities. In their eyes, the lack of respect for nature apparent within these cities is unsettling. But they find peace in the knowledge these settlements won't last forever and that one day they will be taken back by nature.

The elves themselves live in forests. Their homes are made through rituals in order to form living wood structures. They only take from nature what they need, and even though they sometimes kill an animal for resources, they make sure they use everything. Elves are very calm and patient; elves won't let their emotions run out of control. This makes them great fighters as they're always able to assess a situation calmly.

Many elves aren't very fond of other races and only work with them to ensure their own survival, but friendships can grow from these forced instances of cooperation. Out of all the other races, elves tend to prefer Gnomes and Firbolgs, but it's not uncommon to see cooperation, forced or otherwise, between individual elves and one of many of the other races.

# Cultural Quirks
Elves live for centuries, viewing time not as a rushing river, but as a vast, still lake. This gives them the luxury of infinite patience, but it completely skews their priorities.

Elves are obsessed with singular perfection rather than practical transmission. An elven artisan might spend 400 years perfecting the ultimate method of shaping a single marble pillar, but will adamantly refuse to take an apprentice because "nobody has a century to learn the basics."

Elven cities are breathtakingly beautiful, but chronically decaying. A city might boast the most magnificent singing glass spires in the world, but the one guy who knew how to repair the plumbing died two centuries ago, and nobody else bothered to learn because plumbing wasn't "art." As a result, decaying elven structures a common sight to be found throughout the continent.
